# Red Hat Account Signal Intelligence

A seller-facing account intelligence product that converts external enterprise-account events into ranked, Red Hat-relevant seller actions.

## Why it exists
External account news is high-volume and noisy. Sellers need a concise view of what materially changed, why it may matter to Red Hat, and what to validate next.

## Current status
- First proof of concept completed for JPMorgan Chase.
- POC validates signal collection, filtering, Red Hat-motion mapping, ranking, and seller-ready output.
- Product build in progress.
