Guide

How to read this report

What this report is

This report shows which accounts need your attention and why. It pulls data from three places: your team's activity in People.ai, AI-generated insights from Backstory, and public web sources. It then scores and ranks each account so you can focus on what matters most.

How to navigate

The report works like a drill-down. You start at the broadest level of your scope and click into narrower views:

GEOThe broadest grouping. Contains regions.
RegionA group of territories. Click a territory row to go deeper.
TerritoryA group of accounts. Click an account row to see its full detail.
AccountA single customer. Shows the summary, recommended action, and all signals.

Numbers at each level (activities, meetings, scores) are sums or averages of the accounts below. The breadcrumb at the top lets you move back up.

Signal score

A number from 0 to 100. It answers: "how urgently does this account need attention?" Higher means more urgent. The score is the average of all individual signal scores for that account. Accounts with no signals show a dash.

Signals: Act, Watch, Reject

Each signal is a piece of intelligence about an account. The system sorts every signal into one of three buckets:

LabelMeaning
ACTAct on this. The signal is strong, relevant, and worth your time now.
WATCHWorth knowing, but not urgent. Check back later or gather more information.
REJECTNoise. The system found it but decided it is not relevant or useful. Rejected signals are hidden by default.

Where signals come from

Every signal lists its source. There are four types:

SourceWhat it means
derivedCalculated from your team's own activity data in People.ai. Example: "high engagement but no next meeting."
backstory_mcpGenerated by Backstory AI from your CRM and activity history. Example: "executive sponsor changed."
external_publicFound on the public web: news articles, press releases, budget documents, SEC filings. Always includes a link.
peopleai_queryA direct query result from People.ai data. Example: "activity dropped 40% month over month."

Confidence

How certain the system is about a signal. Three levels:

HighBased on clear, direct evidence. Likely accurate.
MediumBased on partial evidence or inference. Probably right, worth checking.
LowWeak evidence. Treat as a lead, not a fact.

Match status

Whether the system could find this account in People.ai. This matters because unmatched accounts have no activity data.

matchedFound. Activity data is available.
ambiguousMultiple possible matches. Data may be incomplete or mixed.
not foundNot found in People.ai. No activity data available.
unresolvedNot yet looked up. The system has not tried to match this account.

Activity trend

Compares recent activity to past activity for an account. Three values:

↑ increasingMore activity recently than before. Engagement is growing.
→ stableAbout the same level. No change.
↓ decliningLess activity recently. Engagement is dropping.

Activity numbers

All activity counts come from People.ai. They include meetings, emails, and other tracked interactions between your team and the account.

Total ActivitiesAll tracked interactions within the query window (shown in the sidebar).
Meetings (30d)Meetings in the last 30 days.
Emails (30d)Emails in the last 30 days.
OpportunitiesOpen opportunities tied to this account.

Enrichment

"Enriched" means the system went beyond basic activity data and pulled deeper insights from Backstory AI: risks, next steps, topics, and company news. Only the top accounts (by priority score) get enriched. Unenriched accounts still have activity data and scores, but lack AI-generated narrative.

Priority score vs. signal score

Two different numbers. Do not confuse them.

Priority ScoreUsed internally to decide which accounts to enrich. Based on activity volume, opportunities, momentum, and recency. You see it in the account sidebar. You do not need to act on it.
Signal ScoreThe main number you see everywhere. It tells you how urgently an account needs your attention based on its signals. This is the one that matters for your work.

Caveats

Every report lists its limits at the bottom of the account view. Common ones:

  • Backstory AI insights cover the last 30 days unless noted otherwise.
  • External research uses only public sources. It cannot see paywalled or internal content.
  • Scores reflect relative importance within this portfolio, not absolute importance across all accounts.
  • Not every account gets enriched. The query window and enrichment limit are shown in each report's metadata.
